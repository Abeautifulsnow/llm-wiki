# Service Accounts

## Policies

Split broad roles across multiple service accounts instead of stacking policies.

Each service account can hold at most one hundred access policies.

## Key rotation

Service account keys must be rotated at least every ninety days.

A disabled service account stops authenticating within five seconds.

Rotation reminders appear in the console seven days before expiry.
