# Webhooks

## Delivery

Webhook endpoints must respond within 10 seconds or the delivery is retried.

Slow endpoints should acknowledge quickly and process the payload asynchronously.

## Signatures

Webhook signatures use HMAC-SHA256 and include a timestamp to prevent replay attacks.

Verify the signature before parsing the payload.
