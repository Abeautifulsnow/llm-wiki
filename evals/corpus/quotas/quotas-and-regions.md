# Quotas and Regions

## Regions

Nimbus is available in 14 regions across 4 continents.

Region choice is permanent for a project; replication does not change the home region.

## Quota management

Default quotas can be raised by filing a support request.

Quota usage is visible in the console and through the `nimbus quota` command.

Quota increases are evaluated against project age and usage history.
